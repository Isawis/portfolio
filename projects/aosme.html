Project: Account overview

Summary

Part of an initiative to migrate corporate customers to a new digital channel, my responsibility was the Account related pages. SEB provided separate channels depending on company size and complexity of needs.

Step one was to build a baseline for all corporate customers, addressing the fundamental, less complex needs often associated with smaller corporates.

Once this foundation was released, iteration would follow, along with phase 2, including more complex functionality including advanced liquidity products.

Goal

This migration posed an opportunity to rethink our services and flows - simplifying journeys, as well as increasing self service ability, satisfying customers and reducing internal support load.

Design process

As this has been a project spanning a large amount of user flows, the design process was iterated several times; each time focusing on a specific task or functionality. This way, it was possible to dig deeper into specific needs rather than just gaining an overall, shallow understanding.

UX Audit

As a start of the project, a UX audit was performed to understand the current flows to create a basis for what could be done better. Since SEB is a large company, a lot of general insights are already available, but it was necessary to dig deeper into the specific flows related to account overview, and how they intersected with other flows, such as payments and product onboarding.
