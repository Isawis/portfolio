Project: Account overview

Summary

Part of an initiative to migrate corporate customers to a new digital channel, my responsibility was the Account related pages. SEB provided separate channels depending on company size and complexity of needs.

Step one was to build a baseline for all corporate customers, addressing the fundamental, less complex needs often associated with smaller corporates.

Once this foundation was released, iteration would follow, along with phase 2, including more complex functionality including advanced liquidity products.

Goal

This migration posed an opportunity to rethink our services and flows - simplifying journeys, as well as increasing self service ability, satisfying customers and reducing internal support load.

Design process

As this has been a project spanning a large amount of user flows, the design process was iterated several times; each time focusing on a specific task or functionality. This way, it was possible to dig deeper into specific needs rather than just gaining an overall, shallow understanding.

UX Audit

As a start of the project, a UX audit was performed to understand the current flows to create a basis for what could be done better. Since SEB is a large company, a lot of general insights are already available, but it was necessary to dig deeper into the specific flows related to account overview, and how they intersected with other flows, such as payments and product onboarding.

User personas

The personas were created based on existing research and insights, as well as interviews with both customers and client service representatives. The personas were used to guide design decisions, ensuring that the needs and goals of the target users were prioritized throughout the design process.

  • Elle

    Business owner

    • Age: 35
    • Occupation: Owner of a small business with ambition to grow.
    • Tech-savvy, but not an expert in finance and dislikes administration and paperwork.
    • Values efficiency and simplicity in financial management. Prefers self service over calling support.

  • Anna

    Accountant

    • Age: 42
    • Occupation: Accountant for multiple small businesses
    • Highly detail-oriented and organized. Frustrated by having to use several different systems
    • Needs to manage multiple accounts and transactions efficiently. Prefers working with the financial data in ERP systems and Excel.

These two personas were based on generic personas already existing within SEB. But for them to be more usable for us, we had to conduct further user interviews to be able to identify their needs and frustrations in the specific user journeys of this project.

Main user flows

  • Account overview flow

    Goal: View balances, account summaries, and quick insights

  • Account details flow

    Goal: Locate and confirm account details such as interests and credits, or account credentials

  • Booked transactions flow

    Goal: Review and filter booked transactions

  • Documents for bookkeeping

    Goal: Export transaction data and monthly account statements
