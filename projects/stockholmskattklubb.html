Project: Stockholms Kattklubb

Summary

Since 2022, I have been the webmaster of Stockholms Kattklubb: A club in Stockholm for cat breeders. I am responsible for posting news from the club board, maintaining the website in general and adding information about our breeders when they want their information added, or when they have a litter for sale.

The website can be found at Stockholmskattklubb.se

Goal

The main purpose of the website is to share news, provide guidance within breeder processes such as registering new litters, but also to provide a place for our members to market their cattery.

Setting up the website

Starting this project, the club was in need of setting up a new website. There was an old one, but this had to be taken down, and my first assigment was to determine what wesite building tool to use. Since I had experience with wordpress from earlier projects, I thought it was a good fit. This also allowed to control our domain and the website content from the same service.

Structuring content​

Plenty of content needed was already available through the old page. But here, my lack of prior involvement in the club (or in cat breeding in general) really helped. I had issues with understanding the page, where to go to find what sort of information, or even understanding instructions provided in the guides. The old page also didn't have a proper news feed, which the club board had pointed out was a requirement.

The first step was to analyze what content we currently had on the old page, and how I could move content to achieve a more intuitive structure. Information about similar things was rearranged to the same pages, and for those cases where it made more sense to have them separate, I made sure to reference the pages with links to ease navigation.

I also read through a lot of the contents and iterated on some of the texts, specifically those that served as guides for complex processes. I tried to ease the language to a more accessible level, as well as make it less ambiguous.

Since WordPress provides a lot of templates and such, not a lot of sketching was made in this project. The UX focus was rather placed on the semantic structure and intuitive flows on the page.

Webpage contents

As mentioned, the website serves a few core purposes which have been reflected through the different pages. They can be simplified into four categories.

News from the board

News from the board is simply a news feed using the basic functionality of posts provided by WordPress. Most often, content is sent by the board in the form of PDFs. If applicable, I rewrite the contents directly into the post and link the document at the end, both to make the information searchable but also more accessible for assistive tools since PDFs tend to introduce a bunch of accessibility issues.

The four most recent news are also featured on the start page to alert users that there's new information without having to navigate to the news feed.

Help and guiding content

The website aims to guide breeders though answering common questions such as how to register net litters, imported cats or similar issues. There are also information pages about the work of the club, its board members and when to contact who. These pages are static and only changed when improvements are made.

Member marketing

Underneath this category, we have the semi-static page of all our member's cattery contact information, categorized by breed since buyers are typically interested in a specific breed when buying. We keep this page clean by removing inactive members, or by updating the contact information per request.

Since this page is semi dynamic, we make use of a grid where it is easy to move each breeder's contact card around in case we need to rearrange to make place for a new breeder or remove one.

Apart from this page, this category also includes the pages "Avelshanar", "Planerade kullar" and "födda kullar". Here, the breeder's can request to have a time limited post published to market a kittens currently for sale or males available for breeding. The contents are sent by email, polished and then posted to the website to be removed after 3 months to keep the page contents relevant.
