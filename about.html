About

I’m a UX designer specializing in digital accessibility, with experience across the full UX process, from research to released product. With several years working experience within disability care, I am painfully aware of the real-world impact of inaccessible design. Not just WCAG and theoretical knowledge.

I contribute to and help build design systems, and use advanced Figma features like variables and component libraries to create efficient, scalable workflows and accessibility-focused assets that help designers consider accessibility, as well as improves handovers to developers. I also have experience of conducting accessibility audits, using both automatic and manual testing methods.

I assist my colleagues through coaching, guidance and resources in the Accessibility field and continuously question inaccessible design around me.
